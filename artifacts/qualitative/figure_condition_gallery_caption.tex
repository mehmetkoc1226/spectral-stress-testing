Illustrative condition gallery for a garbage truck image (global dataset index 2643). (a) Clean input; (b–e) hard low- and high-pass complements at r=10 and r=20; (f–g) Gaussian low- and high-pass complements at r=10; (h–i) Gaussian HP r=20 before and after actual clipping; (j) absolute pixelwise change induced by clipping; (k) radial log-power profile with r=10 and r=20 marked; and (l) hard and Gaussian low-pass transfer functions. The retained input-energy ratios for panels (a–i) are 100.0\%, 95.1\%, 4.9\%, 97.3\%, 2.7\%, 93.1\%, 4.2\%, 2.2\%, and 1.1\%, respectively. For Gaussian HP r=20, 47.5\% of channel values fall outside [0,1] before clipping and the mean absolute clipping change is 0.0252. Signed-linear RGB panels are clipped to [0,1] only for human-readable display; frozen model inference used the original signed tensors. The example was selected among images classified correctly on clean input by all six checkpoints using a fixed SHA-256 ordering. It is illustrative only; quantitative claims use the complete paired analysis.
